Help & support

Does CashBridge handle my money?

No. CashBridge only helps you find people with opposite needs. We never hold, move or guarantee money.

How does matching work?

We pair opposite needs (cash to digital with digital to cash) within ±10% of your amount. Exact amounts come first. Both people must agree.

What does verification mean?

In this prototype accounts are not verified. Real email verification arrives with Firebase. No badge will ever mean identity or finances are verified.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the match from the Matches tab, then tap Report or Block.

Staying safe

Meet only in public places. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, card details or passwords. Confirm payment has arrived before handing over cash. Do not use CashBridge for anything illegal.
